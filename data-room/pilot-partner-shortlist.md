# AGIS Pilot Partner Shortlist

**Bryan Garcia Rodriguez · Pioneer Innovations Collective LLC · 2026-11-26**

**Status: nobody on this list has been contacted.** These are candidates for
a field pilot in a WFP-operating country in Latin America or the Caribbean,
screened for greenhouse relevance, smallholder reach, and WFP/UNICEF adjacency.
Cuba was excluded (US sanctions exposure for a US LLC) and Haiti deprioritized
(operational complexity) despite WFP presence.

## Shortlist

**1. Honduras: Zamorano University (Escuela Agrícola Panamericana).**
University/extension center hosting the USAID Horticulture Innovation Lab
Regional Center for Central America, validating hydroponic vegetable
production, net houses, solar-pump irrigation, and rainwater harvesting.
Serves Honduras and Guatemala smallholders; credible with WFP's Honduras
home-grown school-feeding and cooperative programs. Contact: research
partnerships via zamorano.edu. Sources: UC Davis Horticulture Innovation Lab
center overview; zamorano.edu research booklet.

**2. Guatemala: Asociación Vivamos Mejor / CEDRACC.**
NGO with a rural education and demo center running an agroecological garden,
nursery, and productive units in the Altiplano; documented partner in
WFP-linked resilience and school-feeding value-chain work in Guatemala.
Natural host for a small greenhouse pilot; UNICEF operates in Guatemala.
Contact: vivamosmejor.org / CEDRACC, Sololá.

**3. Caribbean regional: CARDI (Caribbean Agricultural Research and Development Institute).**
CARICOM agricultural research and extension institute, HQ Trinidad, offices
in 14 countries, all covered by WFP's Caribbean Multi-Country Office. Runs an
explicit Protected Agriculture (greenhouse) program for smallholders. Can act
as a regional implementing partner across several WFP-covered countries at
once. Contact: infocentre@cardi.org; Project Implementation Unit
dbesai@cardi.org; +1 (868) 645-1205.

**4. Colombia: AGROSAVIA (Corporación Colombiana de Investigación Agropecuaria).**
Public agricultural research and extension corporation with about 13
experimental stations, including greenhouse and vegetable programs at
Tibaitatá near Bogotá. Colombia hosts WFP's regional HZero Innovation Hub in
Bogotá, and AGROSAVIA/CIAT ecosystem partners are named in HZero's program:
the strongest WFP-adjacent anchor in the region. Contact:
atencionalcliente@agrosavia.co; +57 601 914 4677.

**5. Ecuador: INIAP (Instituto Nacional de Investigaciones Agropecuarias).**
National agricultural research institute with seven experimental stations
(including Santa Catalina, Pichincha) that host pilots and training. Also a
UNICEF programme country with WFP country-office presence. Contact:
info@iniap.gob.ec / capacitacion@iniap.gob.ec; +593 2 256 7645.

**6. Peru: Universidad Nacional Agraria La Molina (UNALM).**
University; its Centro de Investigación de Hidroponía y Nutrición Mineral and
horticulture research group run a 10 ha experimental site and work with
smallholder horticulture in coastal valleys. Near-perfect technical match for
an irrigation controller pilot. Contact: huerto@lamolina.edu.pe /
delfin@lamolina.edu.pe (CIHNM); +51 348 5796.

**7. Dominican Republic: IDIAF (Instituto Dominicano de Investigaciones Agropecuarias y Forestales).**
State agricultural research and extension institute with centers in La Vega,
San Juan de la Maguana, and Pedro Brand; does technology validation and
transfer to producers. WFP operates in the Dominican Republic. Contact:
idiaf@idiaf.gov.do; +1 (809) 567-8999.

**8. Regional: IICA (Inter-American Institute for Cooperation on Agriculture).**
Hemispheric technical-cooperation body with delegations in nearly every WFP
country; signed a formal cooperation agreement with WFP Honduras to
strengthen smallholder cooperatives under Purchase for Progress. Can convene
cooperatives to host a pilot and serves as a multi-country implementing
partner. Contact: iica.int country delegations.

## Selection notes

- Every country above is also a UNICEF programme country; CARDI and IICA are
  multi-country, so one partnership could cover both a WFP-track pilot and a
  future UNICEF Venture Fund application.
- The WFP Innovation Accelerator's live form only considers applicants
  already operating in a WFP country, including through implementing
  partners: securing one of these partners is the gating step for the held
  application.

## Recommended order of approach

1. **Zamorano**: most direct technical and smallholder fit, single country.
2. **AGROSAVIA**: strongest WFP adjacency via the Bogotá HZero hub.
3. **CARDI or IICA**: if a multi-country umbrella is preferred over a
   single-site pilot.

Order to be confirmed with Bryan before first contact.
