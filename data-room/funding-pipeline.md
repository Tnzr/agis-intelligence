# AGIS Funding Pipeline

**Bryan Garcia Rodriguez · Pioneer Innovations Collective LLC · 2026-11-26**

AGIS's funding path is non-dilutive first: UN and climate grants plus
open-hardware crowdfunding, per the Pioneer Innovations funding roadmap
(2026-11-25). No equity raise is planned before pilot evidence exists.

## Why the applications are held

Both drafts are complete except for facts only real-world progress can
supply: an operating country, an MVP verified in the field, measured
outcomes, and a costed budget. Submitting with gaps filled by estimates would
fail the forms' own eligibility language and weaken every later application.
The gating sequence is: partner confirmed, hardware validated, pilot run,
then submit.

## Prerequisites in progress or pending

- **Pilot partner**: shortlist ready (`pilot-partner-shortlist.md`); no
  contact made yet.
- **Hardware test**: firmware must run on a real ESP32, pump, fan, and flow
  meter before any field claim.
- **Costed BOM**: a pilot-country bill of materials and installed cost; the
  historical $173.87/$404.85 parts totals are not quotes.
- **SAM.gov / Grants.gov / eRA Commons** registrations for Pioneer
  Innovations Collective LLC: needed for federal grant submissions
  (4–6 week lead time per the funding roadmap).
- **Refreshed pitch deck** (10 slides max for WFP): in production with
  Pioneer Innovations' Pitch Desk bot.

## Revenue model, as proposed

Keep software and hardware designs open; sell a controller kit for growers
who prefer not to source and assemble parts, plus optional installation and
maintenance support. Grants fund validation and training, not ongoing
operations. This is a proposed model to test through crowdfunding and the
pilot; no sales or crowdfunding have occurred.
